*Digest — 2026-08-19*

_TL;DR: Apple patches a zero-click image bug, Claude beats human hit-rates on protein design, Pennsylvania makes AI data centers pay their own grid costs, and capital keeps moving into H200 chips and Bitcoin treasuries._

1. *Apple patches a zero-click ImageIO code-execution bug*
   iOS 26.6.1, iPadOS 26.6.1, and macOS Tahoe 26.6.2 fix CVE-2026-65346, an ImageIO integer-overflow where just rendering a malicious image can trigger code execution — found by Meta's Red Team X.
   Why it matters: No known in-the-wild exploitation yet, but zero-click image bugs are prime spyware material — update today.
   https://www.forbes.com/sites/kateoflahertyuk/2026/08/19/ios-2661-and-ios-18710-update-now-warning-for-all-iphone-users/

2. *Claude autonomously designed 354 working protein binders*
   Anthropic had Claude Opus 4.8 run existing protein-design and co-folding models end-to-end across 15 drug targets (PD-L1, EGFR, TNFα, others), generating 1,320 candidates. Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience lab-tested them: 354 bound (22.6–35.1% hit rate vs. the field's usual 10–15%).
   Why it matters: Lab-verified, not a benchmark claim — a real jump in de novo drug-discovery throughput.
   https://www.anthropic.com/research/Claude-accelerates-protein-design

3. *Pennsylvania signs the country's strictest AI data-center rules*
   Shapiro's order pulls AI data centers from Fast Track permitting, bans data-center NDAs, requires local approval before construction, and makes developers over 25MW cover their own grid-upgrade costs plus clean power.
   Why it matters: 100+ PA data-center proposals were in talks — this resets their economics and could become a template other states copy.
   https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2026-press-releases/governor-shapiro-signs-executive-order-on-data-center-developmen

4. *Nvidia's H200 chips are flowing into China again*
   ByteDance and Tencent have each reportedly received ~10,000 H200 units in recent weeks under the revised US license-review policy — sales stay capped at 50% of volume sold to US customers, plus certification.
   Why it matters: Reopens a multi-billion-dollar China channel for Nvidia, though still short of pre-restriction access.
   https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/08/61293582/nvidias-h200-chips-are-flowing-into-china-again-bytedance-tencent-get-around-10000-each-report-says

5. *Metaplanet commits 2,100 BTC to launch a US Bitcoin treasury platform*
   Metaplanet will contribute 2,100 BTC (~$132M) plus $2.5M cash into Nasdaq-listed Super League Enterprise, taking a 95.7% stake and renaming it Superplanet (ticker SUPA); closing expected Q4 2026. Metaplanet already holds ~43,000 BTC.
   Why it matters: A second US Bitcoin-treasury vehicle for Metaplanet — watch SUPA/SLE around the Q4 close.
   https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/18/3346825/0/en/metaplanet-to-invest-2-100-bitcoin-in-super-league-to-launch-u-s-bitcoin-treasury-platform-superplanet.html
